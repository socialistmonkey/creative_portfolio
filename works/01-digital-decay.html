Digital Decay
Video (mp4) / Editor & videographer — Adobe After Effects, Datamosh 2
CONCEPT
This experimental video project explores the synthesis of disparate media through the lens of digital decay, blending cultural artifacts such as cinema, video games, anime, and mobile video applications with a personal footage archive. Datamoshing serves as both the transition and the unifying power; pixels bleed from one frame to the next, breaking down traditional cuts and replacing them with a continuous stream of visual data corruption. The work also utilizes a track by a Georgian artist. The later part of the video, where personal videos start popping up, is presented as a recollection of memories and interesting visuals, which then get lost in the background pixels and merge all together as the music settles down and the ambience takes over.
TECHNICAL
The entire work was completed in Adobe After Effects with the help of the Datamosh 2 plugin, which is quite taxing on the hardware since datamoshing requires a lot of mathematical calculations. The project heavily relied on cuts, layering and blending modes to achieve the desired final product. The memory recollection sequence required manual timing of each clip to ensure it would pop up on the screen at the correct time. A minimal amount of audio mixing was done to lower the music track as the ambience swells up towards the end. Featured media credits: My Dinner with Andre, 2001: A Space Odyssey, Hotline Miami, Perfect Blue, Monovista, "Fireworks" by Dillanext.
