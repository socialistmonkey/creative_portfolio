Gorbachev x Louis Vuitton
Poster (png, 9:16) / Graphic designer — Adobe Photoshop
CONCEPT
This satirical poster highlights the duality and political irony of Mikhail Gorbachev's legacy. It contrasts his role as a communist Soviet Union leader with his 2007 Louis Vuitton commercial and the less controversial, but still relevant, story of bringing Pizza Hut into the USSR market. The iconic Louis Vuitton pattern has been put on his shirt as well, set against a background of a rundown house with the same pattern graffitied onto it. Several versions of the subject also emphasize the contradictions of Gorbachev. Textured Chinese text adds a critical message, which might not appear obvious but translates to: "Gorbachev was fundamentally not a true revolutionary." The USSR TV logo is placed in the corner as well.
TECHNICAL
Designed entirely using Adobe Photoshop, the piece combines digital collage with rough textures for a gritty, raw style. The iconic pattern was placed on the shirt precisely using masking and an online-sourced transparent PNG. Each subject had its own distinct style, using different photo-filter effects: mosaic pixelation, posterization, and hue tweaks.
