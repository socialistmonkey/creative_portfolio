A Movement for Literacy and Equality Buried by Empires and Extremism
Poster series (jpg · 3 images) / Graphic designer — Adobe Photoshop
CONCEPT
Three posters built around the Saur Revolution and what it promised but never delivered: literacy, women's rights, justice and resistance to tyranny. The large Persian typography pulled directly from PDPA declarations carries most of the visual weight. The mosaic filter runs through the whole series as a stand-in for how mainstream media flattens and reduces the Afghan population into a single image. The poppy appears across all the pieces as a symbol of wartime grief, rooted in Afghan cultural memory rather than the Western drug-trade reading most people default to.
TECHNICAL
Built using Adobe Photoshop. Layering has a significant role across all three pieces, alongside masking of the individual subjects. Filters such as noise, mosaic, Gaussian blur and sharpen were applied. All three have different aspect ratios: one is 9:16 to accommodate the mobile phone standard, while the others are 16:9 for the landscape ratio and 2:3 for the classical poster ratio. Non-standard Persian typographies were used as well.
