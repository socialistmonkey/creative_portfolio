“Wach Auf” (Work in Progress)
Music video, WIP (mp4) / Video editor — Adobe Premiere Pro, Adobe After Effects
CONCEPT
This work-in-progress project transforms a short film shot in the style of a 1920s German silent film into a music video for a Georgian-American musician, intended for submission to the Berlin Music Video Awards. Editing from raw and alternative takes, the current version establishes a demo cut across the first four scenes. Era-appropriate dialogue cards are implemented to anchor the aesthetic. To introduce a new scene, a creative transition was designed where the black void around the title expands inward, revealing the next environment to create an engaging visual experience. Additionally, isolated character cutouts from alternative scenes are layered into the edit to generate mystery surrounding the appearance of a new character.
TECHNICAL
The production workflow consists of asset selection, sequential assembly, and visual effects integration, all precisely synchronized to the accompanying audio track. The primary editing and assembly are managed within Adobe Premiere Pro, while complex visual effects are engineered in Adobe After Effects and imported back into the main timeline. Advanced rotoscoping is utilized across several sequences to generate distinct visual layers. Furthermore, a lens distortion effect serves as a spatial transitional mechanism to warp perspectives between scenes. Animating the typography's structural outline was also required to seamlessly execute the title sequence's visual elements.
