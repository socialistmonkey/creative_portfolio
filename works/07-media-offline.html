Media Offline
Experimental reel (mp4, 9:16) / Video editor — Pixelcrash.xyz, Adobe Premiere Pro
CONCEPT
This experimental reel explores video processing through ASCII text formatting and glitch aesthetics. The edit uses a bit of humor by incorporating Premiere Pro's infamous "Media Offline" warning screen in multiple languages to mess with the viewer's expectations, paired with a matching auditory effect. It was also important to adapt the 9:16 aspect ratio for social media.
TECHNICAL
The experimental short-form reel was built using the browser-based tool Pixelcrash and Adobe Premiere Pro. The final product maps text characters onto raw video frames based on brightness and edge detection in real time. By adjusting the scan direction, characters, colors, and cell density, the original mundane footage becomes one with the digital text. Final adjustments included adding a lens distortion effect as a transitional element and frequent cuts to reinforce the chaotic and glitchy atmosphere. For the audio, stem separation was necessary on the track "The Most Dear and the Future" by EAR to remove the vocals, leaving only the instrumental rhythm.
