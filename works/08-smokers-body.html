The Smoker's Body
Poster (png, 9:16) / Graphic designer — Adobe Photoshop
CONCEPT
This experimental poster comes from a fascination with a graphic, vintage anti-smoking advertisement found online. While the original commercial was designed to warn the public of the risks caused by cigarettes, my intention was to lean heavily into body and digital horror aesthetics. The goal was to subvert the public health warning into something surreal and unsettling, to provoke an immediate gut reaction from the viewer, with the addition of uncanny jaws on the top and bottom and raw flesh as the background. A texture was applied over the whole image to introduce an analog horror feel.
TECHNICAL
The project was created entirely inside Adobe Photoshop using precise masking and texture blending. The central vintage advertisement asset was isolated by keying out the white color of the original poster. Terrifying teeth were isolated and arranged on the top and bottom edges of the canvas to serve as a frame. A texture was applied using the "Darken" blend mode.
