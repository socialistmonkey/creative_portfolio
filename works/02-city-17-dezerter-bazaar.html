City 17: Dezerter Bazaar
Vertical reel (mp4, 9:16) / Videographer & editor — Adobe Premiere Pro, Adobe Photoshop
CONCEPT
This experimental digital video project merges the gritty, dystopian atmosphere of the video game Half-Life 2 with the chaotic reality of Tbilisi's Deserter Bazaar. The reel uses absurdist humor to turn a mundane, frantic urban environment into an interactive game level. The inclusion of the classic Half-Life 2 heads-up display (HUD), the soundtrack, and weapons completely recontextualizes the space. Equipped items switch dynamically based on real-world environmental cues, like approaching individuals or oncoming cars, while camera bobbing mimics the first-person player perspective.
TECHNICAL
The original footage was shot handheld while walking through the bazaar and edited using Adobe Premiere Pro. Green-screen video game assets were sourced online and keyed into the composition using the Ultra Key effect. The audio design mixes raw street audio with the official Half-Life 2 soundtrack and the game's actual sound effects to ground the illusion. Finally, the project utilizes a vertical 9:16 aspect ratio to conform to modern short-form media and reel standards.
