SpaceX Commercial
Motion design (mp4) / Motion designer & sequence animator — Adobe Illustrator, Adobe After Effects
CONCEPT
This is an example commercial for SpaceX that I made while studying animation using After Effects, functioning as a major academic assignment. It maps a narrative timeline of an individual's aspiration toward aerospace engineering and deep-space exploration. We follow a person's life from childhood to the point of them reaching their goal, and each major step is animated. Traditional cuts are bridged by zooming directly into the infinite-resolution boundaries of vector shapes to create smooth transitions. The audio track has been chosen thematically as well, placing the Outer Wilds soundtrack as the musical orchestration of the animation, conceptually mirroring the narrative's underlying tension between space exploration and existential isolation.
TECHNICAL
The production pipeline utilized Adobe Illustrator to dissect and isolate flat vector graphics before staging them across seven interconnected compositions within Adobe After Effects. Given the complexity of navigating multiple nested pre-compositions, strict layer hierarchy and file organization were crucial to maintaining an efficient workflow. The 45-second sequence utilizes seamless positional, scaling, and rotational spatial adjustments. Technical execution involved anchoring motion paths to null objects to replicate multi-plane, dynamic camera movements, and using the puppet pin tool to add organic secondary animation to characters. The workflow also included creating looping animations for specific scenes, stacking turbulent displacement and mosaic filters to execute a stylized digital scan-line transition, and a custom reveal animation designed for the final branding sequence.
