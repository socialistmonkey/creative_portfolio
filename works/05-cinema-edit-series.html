Cinema Edit Series
Cinema edit series (mp4 × 2) / Video editor — Adobe Premiere Pro, Adobe After Effects
CONCEPT
This series of two film cinema edits combines my appreciation for Midnight in Paris (2011) and Marie Antoinette (2006) with tracks by Daft Punk and Charli XCX, serving as a technical experiment in visual manipulation. While both films are beautiful, the core idea was to build scenes around the chosen musical tracks, and both tracks were chosen accordingly and intentionally. For the Midnight in Paris edit, Daft Punk's "Something About Us" was chosen because its slow, romantic piano and soft melancholy perfectly match Woody Allen's narrative; both the film's setting and the electronic duo also share a connection to Paris. The Marie Antoinette edit matches the film's extravagant party scenes with the lyrics of Charli XCX's "Party 4U," portraying the historical figure as a reckless teenager. This later edit also adapts the style of the Brat album cover, placing the lyrics at the center with the exact same styling and typography.
TECHNICAL
Adobe After Effects was used across both cinema edits, while Adobe Premiere Pro was additionally used to assemble the scenes for the "Marie Antoinette" edit. Both workflows involved rotoscoping to isolate characters. Posterization of time was used to mimic retro cameras during the cancan scene, alongside adjustments to grain, exposure, and color. Dehancer Pro v2.0 was implemented to apply warm, washed-out film profiles, while precise match-cuts created smooth transitions synchronized with the track's changing mood.
