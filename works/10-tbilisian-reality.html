Tbilisian Reality
Poster series (jpg · 2 images, 9:16) / Photographer & graphic designer — Adobe Photoshop
CONCEPT
This two-part poster series pulls from Tbilisi's late-night streets: confrontations, gambling dens, pawnshops, the kind of dark humor you only really get if you're local. Built from original street photography, it skips the postcard version of the city on purpose. What I wanted was something more raw, a visual argument about the friction of everyday urban life here, right now. Georgian typography carries most of the concept; the letterforms are modified and shift tone from piece to piece, sometimes sharp and aggressive like tabloid crime headlines.
TECHNICAL
Every image runs through heavy grain, pixelation, and mosaic distortion, treatments that end up feeling closer to surveillance footage or a half-redacted file than a normal photo. On top of that I layered in video game HUD elements, which blurs the line between documentary photo and interface. To keep the series feeling like one body of work rather than 2 separate images, the layer styling stays consistent throughout: the same drop shadows, inner glows, and bevel-and-emboss combination on every digital asset. It gives otherwise flat images a bit of physical weight.
