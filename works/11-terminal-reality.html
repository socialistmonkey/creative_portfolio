Terminal Reality
Experimental reel (mp4, 9:16 · 2 clips) / Videographer & editor — Adobe After Effects
CONCEPT
This 15-second experimental reel explores the synthesis of real-world videography and digital text environments (ASCII characters). The project consists of three pieces of original footage: a walk through the Holocaust Memorial in Berlin, an arcade machine in Tbilisi, and an underground Tbilisi clothing market. This work was created as visual experimentation and a dedicated learning opportunity. Originally the entire video was in ASCII, but a transition from real footage into an ASCII world was added, and towards the end, in an underground tunnel, those two worlds merge. The musical orchestration changes and becomes more hectic as more movement is introduced in the video as well. The final segment layers the raw video and ASCII render together, blending physical forms with text aesthetics.
TECHNICAL
The video was built entirely using Adobe After Effects, utilizing original footage captured on-site. To transition into the digital landscape of ASCII characters, datamoshing was utilized as the primary transitional mechanism. Using the Extract effect, specific luminance ranges were isolated from the video data to map unique characters to their corresponding brightness levels, which were then tied to the footage using track mattes. For the audio, the song "Red Weather" by ONDA ANDAR was split into instrumental stems to isolate the instrumental and remove the vocals. Finally, the project was adapted to a 9:16 aspect ratio to align with the technical standards of modern short-form content consumption.
