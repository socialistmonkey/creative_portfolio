Tbilisi Silk Road Forum Motion Design
Motion design (mp4 · 3 clips) / Motion designer & asset pipeline editor — Adobe Illustrator, Adobe After Effects
CONCEPT
This commercial motion design involved translating static branding assets into an ambient, high-scale environmental animation for one of the largest corporate business conferences in Georgia. Conceptual symbolism was structurally integrated to mirror the themes of the forum, implementing a rhythmic pulse on the corporate identity logo to mimic a heartbeat, signifying continuous economic vitality. Accompanying graphic components on the periphery were animated in opposing linear directions to map the historical and contemporary bilateral trade routes of the Silk Road.
TECHNICAL
All of the work was completed using Adobe Illustrator and Adobe After Effects. The workflow consisted of dissecting multi-layered vector source graphics inside Illustrator before importing them into After Effects. To maintain visual subtlety appropriate for the occasion, animations were automated via the expression text field and graph editor, combining precise keyframe parameters across scale, opacity, and rotation into an uninterrupted, looping sequence.
